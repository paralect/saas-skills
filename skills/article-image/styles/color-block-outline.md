STYLE: a flat color block poster. 1 saturated field, 1 white outlined object floating in it, nothing else.

- The background color fills the entire canvas, edge to edge and corner to corner. No frame, no border, no rounded corners, no inner panel, no margin in another color.
- No gradient, no texture, no shading anywhere in the image.
- The subject is clean line art: pure white fills with thin even black contour lines, in axonometric projection, floating in the middle of the field. No ground plane, no cast shadow, no vanishing point.
- Interior detail is minimal, a few black lines for structure only. The object reads as a diagram of itself, not a rendering.
- At most 2 accent marks: a thin bright wireframe box drawn around the part the post is about, and a small rounded label tag with 1 lowercase word in it. The accent color input picks their color.
- The background color comes from this set: marigold yellow, grass green, crimson red, cobalt blue, cyan, magenta.
- No text of any kind, whatever the title lettering input says. The only word in the frame is the 1 inside the label tag. No caption, no band along any edge, no window chrome, no corner dots.
