STYLE: hand drawn black ink explainer sketch, the kind a founder draws on a whiteboard. Not a polished illustration.

- Pure black ink line art on plain white background. No color, no gradients, no flat fills. This style is monochrome, so ignore the accent color input.
- Loose, slightly imperfect hand drawn lines. Not vector smooth, not geometrically perfect. Corners can overshoot. Lines have natural wobble.
- Minimal cross hatching, used only as small shadow accents on 2 or 3 edges, never all over.
- People are the simplest possible stick figures: circle head, 2 dots for eyes, 1 curved line for a mouth. No other detail.
- Abstract things get iconic containers: a cylinder for stored data, boxes for categories or products, a cloud for an idea, a jagged starburst for a payoff, simple bar charts for scale.
- Relationships are hand drawn arrows: curved, single stroke, sometimes crossing the whole canvas.
- Composition is asymmetric. Not centered, not grid aligned.
- Lettering is hand printed in the same rough ink as the drawing, never a clean font, 1 to 3 words per label.
