STYLE: an axonometric desk scene lit by 1 pool of light, everything flat, everything cropped.

- Looking down on a desk in axonometric projection. Objects are flat shapes with no outline strokes, just occasional thin lighter edge highlights.
- The field is a dark desaturated navy. Objects around the edge are cropped by the frame: a laptop, a monitor, a notebook, a stack of paper, an envelope. None of them is fully in frame, which is what makes it read as a real desk rather than a diagram.
- A large pale ellipse of light lies across the middle like a spotlight. Everything inside it shifts to a lighter tint of its own color, and an object crossing the edge changes tint mid shape, as though a translucent gel were laid over it. That boundary is the strongest thing in the composition, so the subject sits on it.
- Fine grain sits on the ellipse and on the larger colored surfaces. Nothing is glossy, nothing carries a gradient ramp.
- 5 colors only: dark navy, slate blue, cornflower blue, cream white, and the accent color, which belongs to the product object and nothing else.
- Floating above the desk, 2 or 3 small white rounded cards tilted into the same projection, each holding 1 tiny icon, plus 1 larger card that reads as a real notification with 2 short lines in it.
- Type appears only inside those cards, small, and it is the only text in the image.
