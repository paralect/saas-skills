STYLE: a technical blueprint schematic, drawn as if the idea were a machine on a drafting sheet.

- Deep navy blueprint paper with a faint grid, a soft vignette, and 1 or 2 subtle fold creases.
- Every line is thin pale cyan white drafting line work. Outlines only, no fills, no shading, no gradients.
- Drafting conventions are visible and doing real work: dimension lines with arrowheads at both ends, dashed hidden lines, centre lines, small circled callout numbers, short leader lines to labels.
- The accent color is amber orange, on exactly 1 part of the drawing: the part the post is about.
- Labels are hand lettered drafting capitals, 1 or 2 words each, small. Never a sentence.
- Title lettering, if any, is the same drafting capitals, larger, underlined, across the top.
- Nothing glows. No neon, no bloom, no 3D render.
