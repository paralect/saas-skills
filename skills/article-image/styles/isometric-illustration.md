STYLE: a flat isometric line illustration, the house style of a modern product blog.

- True isometric projection on 30 degree axes. No perspective convergence, no vanishing point.
- Thin dark ink outlines of even weight throughout. Flat faces, no gradients, no texture, no shading ramps.
- 3 values only: off white top faces, light warm grey side faces, mid grey for the darkest planes. The accent color is the 4th, on exactly 1 element.
- Background is a plain very light warm grey field. Objects float on it with a soft flat grey shadow directly beneath.
- People are simplified figures with rounded featureless heads, blocky torsos, no facial detail. Posture does all the acting.
- No blurred shadows, no glossy highlights, no ambient occlusion. Nothing that suggests a 3D render.
- Lettering, if any, is a clean geometric sans in the same dark ink, small, 1 or 2 words.
