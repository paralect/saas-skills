STYLE: large simple shapes filled with smooth duotone gradients, under heavy film grain.

- Heavy grain over the entire frame, coarse and clearly visible, the way a risograph or a grainy poster print looks.
- Nothing is a flat color. Every shape and the field itself carry a gradient blending 2 or 3 hues. Electric blue into orange on near black, or blue into pink on pale blush.
- Shapes are large, simple and semi-abstract, overlapping, set at a slight angle: slabs, spheres, frames, a hand, a burst. 3 or 4 of them, no more.
- Detail is hairline strokes in a single hue: wireframe rectangles, arcs, tick marks, small square selection handles, a cursor arrow. Uniform hairline weight throughout.
- 1 soft radial bloom sits behind the subject in the brightest hue. No lens flare.
- No outlines around the large shapes, no drop shadows, no text of any kind, whatever the title lettering input says.
